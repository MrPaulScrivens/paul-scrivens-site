sec. 01the power law

Sixteen posts — 0.8% of everything I shipped — earned 62.3% of every view.

Meanwhile 40% of my output lives under 300 views and adds up to 4.3% of the total. Half of everything I wrote landed under 363.

That’s the actual shape of a content account. The feed shows you the sixteen. The other 1,907 are the part nobody screenshots.

sec. 06the read

Reach is bought at the first line. Question and number openers carry a ~30% premium and sit in under 20% of my output. The reversal move — my biggest ceiling — got used 8 times in two years.

Volume was the wrong lever. My highest-output quarter produced my worst medians. The rep that compounds is the opener, and shipping more of the same opener is the same rep.

The floor is family and the ceiling is history. Family scenes: 984 median, 16 uses. History reversals: 230k best, 8 uses. I know exactly what next month’s posts look like. Whether I write them is a different problem.
