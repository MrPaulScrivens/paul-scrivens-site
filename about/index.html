about paul

sec. 01the short version

I’m Paul. I run seven brands by myself — otters, zebras, yaks, sloths, a noodle, an underachiever, and this one. Six of them wear costumes. I make the costumes.

I haven’t worked for someone else in over fifteen years. I’m the sole breadwinner for my family, which means the whole operation has one rule: it has to actually make money, not just look busy.

I have ADHD. I spent a long time treating that like the problem, and now it’s the reason the portfolio exists — a brain that was never going to pick one thing, given seven things instead.

sec. 02how it runs

No team. No agency behind the curtain. The whole thing runs on plain text files, a terminal, and a filing system that holds it together when my attention doesn’t.

Each brand has its own voice, its own audience, and its own money. None of them mention me. This site is where the costumes come off and the numbers get read out loud — like the threads ledger, where I published the full stats of two years of posting, including the fourteen hundred posts that went nowhere.

I didn’t start qualified. At 22 I defaulted on five credit cards. Most of what I know got learned the expensive way, which is why the letters lean on receipts instead of advice.

sec. 03the letters

One email, most weeks. What got built across the seven brands, what it made, what it cost to learn. The good months and the embarrassing ones.

No course waiting at the end. It’s a letter.
