paul scrivens letters the brands about

I run five brands at once. On purpose.

Otters, zebras, yaks, sloths, and one underachiever. Different voices, different looks, different money. None of them know the others exist.

Why five? Because I have the kind of brain that was never going to pick one thing. I spent years treating that like a problem to fix. Now it’s the whole operation.

the letters

One email, most weeks. What got built, what it made, what it cost to learn.

Numbers included. The good months and the embarrassing ones.

No course waiting at the end. It’s a letter.

the brands

Five so far. Each one runs its own world. I just work here.

wobbly otters
The membership. One-person businesses building a content machine out of plain text files.
talented underachiever
For smart people getting out-earned by dumber ones. Yes, it stings. It’s supposed to.
happy zebras
Social content, done for you, so business owners can get back to the part they actually like.
bumbling yaks
Astrology and human design for people who lost the gratitude journal. The chart runs anyway.
drooling sloths
AI agents installed into small businesses. The board stays green. The client stays asleep.

The letters cover what it takes to run all of them without a team. Mostly the mistakes.
