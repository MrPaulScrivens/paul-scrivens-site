sec. 01the operation

Otters, zebras, yaks, sloths, an underachiever. The list keeps growing.

Different voices, different money. None of them know the others exist.

Why so many? Because I have the kind of brain that was never going to pick one thing.

I spent years treating that like a problem to fix. Now it’s the whole operation.

No team. No agency behind the curtain. One person, a closet full of costumes, and a filing system that holds it together.
